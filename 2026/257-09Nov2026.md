## Automated Snowpipe Setup with Snowflake CoCo (GA)

Go from raw files in S3, Azure Blob or GCS to a continuously loading Snowflake or Snowflake-managed Iceberg table in one natural-language conversation. CoCo samples files, infers the schema, builds the pipeline and validates loads. On AWS, a single time-limited IAM temporary delegation request lets Snowflake create the storage role, trust policy and bucket notifications with no access keys shared. CoCo can also generate Terraform, CloudFormation or ARM templates. Bulk loading with CoCo is GA too.
https://www.snowflake.com/en/blog/automated-snowpipe-setup-snowflake-coco/

https://docs.snowflake.com/en/user-guide/data-load-snowpipe-auto-coco
