## How Thrive Learning Improved Dynamic Tables Price-Performance by 2X

Thrive Learning cut Dynamic Table costs from hundreds of credits/day to ~7 credits/day using Custom Incrementalization (REFRESH_MODE = CUSTOM_INCREMENTAL). Auto-clustering dropped from ~150 to ~2 credits/day. Transformation compute dropped from ~150 to ~5 credits/day. The pipeline stayed in dbt lineage with no separate orchestration layer.
https://www.snowflake.com/en/blog/thrive-dynamic-table-costs-custom-incremental/
