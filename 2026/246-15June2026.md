## **Tim's Corner: Snowflake Summit 2026 Recap - The Agentic Data Cloud is Here**

Welcome to issue 246 - the **Snowflake Summit 2026 Recap Edition!** What a week in San Francisco! Summit 2026 delivered on every front: AI agents, open data, and governance took center stage. The announcements were massive - **Claude Fable 5** on Cortex AI, **OpenAI partnership** for business-native AI, **AI Smart Pipelines**, **Cortex Code Desktop with MCP and dbt panel**, **Snowflake Optima** for query performance planning, **Online Feature Store** with stream feature views, **SPCS AI_COMPLETE and AI_EMBED** in containers, **DCM Projects**, **Batch Inference Jobs** for vision models, and **Semantic Layer for AI Risk in Finance**. The engineering team published on efficient ingestion patterns, context caching, and scaling price-performant AI inference. Plus partner awards, distributed medical image processing with MONAI, and Tim's Summit talk video is live! The momentum continues with hands-on labs and webinars coming up. Let's dig into everything.
