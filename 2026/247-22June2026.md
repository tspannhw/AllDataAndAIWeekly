## **Tim's Corner: Post-Summit Momentum - Adaptive Compute, Dynamic Tables, and Streaming at Scale**

Welcome to issue 247! The post-Summit momentum continues this week with major GA announcements: **Adaptive Compute** is generally available, **Dynamic Tables** get faster and more flexible, **Postgres Data Mirroring** lands, and **Snowpipe Streaming** gets a deep-dive on real-time pipelines. The community delivered incredible content - from streaming 2.5 GB/s of connected vehicle data into Snowflake, to advanced multi-tenant semantic layer patterns, to best practices from a year of AI-augmented development with Cortex Code. On the AI side, **Cortex Agent Tool Metrics** gives visibility into agent behavior, **Snowflake Cowork Artifacts** are GA, and the Snowflake AI Kit now supports Claude Code. Plus new hands-on labs scheduled through August, Tim's Conf42 LLM talk on SQL automation, and a wealth of community repos. Let's dig in.
